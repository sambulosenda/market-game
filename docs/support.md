## Common questions

**My purchase did not arrive.**
Close the game fully and open it again while online. If the gems are still missing, open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. If that does not help, email us with the receipt that Apple or Google sent you.

**How do I get a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). On Android, go to your order history in Google Play.

**Does the game need the internet?**
No. You can play offline. You need a connection only to buy gems and to back up your game.

**Will I lose my street if I change phones?**
When you are online, the game backs up your street. Install the game on the new phone and it offers to bring the street back. If two phones have different streets, the game asks which one to keep.

**How do I turn off notifications?**
Open Settings in the game and switch Notifications off. The game sends at most two a day and none at night.

**How do I delete my data?**
Open Settings in the game and tap Delete my data. This removes your street from the phone and from our backup. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

**I found a bug.**
Please email us what happened and, if you can, a screenshot.
